### Commit 26 AVX-512 Encode Evidence

Primary implementation host: AMD Ryzen 9 9950X3D, 16 cores/32 threads, Linux
x86-64, with `avx512f`, `avx512bw`, `avx512vl`, and `avx512vbmi` reported by the
kernel. Direct checked and unchecked static `no_std` execution passes for
SSSE3/SSE4.1, AVX2, and AVX-512 VBMI. Focused exact-backend measurements support
the conservative 192-byte automatic AVX-512 crossover on this host.

Pending before the 2.0 release candidate: rerun the Commit 26 focused campaign
on a second, preferably Intel, AVX-512 VBMI microarchitecture. Until that record
exists, documentation may describe the selected policy and this AMD result but
must not generalize throughput or frequency behavior across AVX-512 CPUs.
