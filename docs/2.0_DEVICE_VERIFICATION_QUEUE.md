### Commit 26 AVX-512 Encode Evidence

Primary implementation host: AMD Ryzen 9 9950X3D, 16 cores/32 threads, Linux
x86-64, with `avx512f`, `avx512bw`, `avx512vl`, and `avx512vbmi` reported by the
kernel. Direct checked and unchecked static `no_std` execution passes for
SSSE3/SSE4.1, AVX2, and AVX-512 VBMI. Focused exact-backend measurements support
the conservative 192-byte automatic AVX-512 crossover on this host.

Deferred corroboration for 2.0.1: rerun the Commit 26 focused campaign on a
second, preferably Intel, AVX-512 VBMI microarchitecture. This is not represented
as 2.0.0 evidence. Until that record exists, documentation may describe the
selected policy and this AMD result but must not generalize throughput or
frequency behavior across AVX-512 CPUs.

### Commit 34 AVX-512 Decode Decision

Primary implementation host: the same AMD Ryzen 9 9950X3D and complete
AVX-512 VBMI feature bundle. Exhaustive direct-kernel tests and checked and
unchecked static `no_std` execution pass. The retained Commit 34 15-sample
campaign did not preserve the provisional automatic crossover: the weakest
AVX-512/AVX2 median ratio was 1.0166, below the frozen 1.02 requirement.
Automatic strict decode therefore remains AVX2; AVX-512 is exact/static only.

Future 2.0.1 reconsideration requires running
`BASE64_NG_RUN_COMMIT28_PERF=1 scripts/check-2.0-x86-decode-hot-paths.sh` on a
second, preferably Intel, AVX-512 VBMI microarchitecture and retaining the exact
CPU/toolchain/evidence record. No automatic crossover or portable AVX-512
performance/frequency claim exists in the frozen matrix.

accepted, public AArch64 dispatch remains admitted NEON or scalar.
